A benchmark derived from BrowseComp, designed to evaluate agents' ability to answer multi-hop queries over a large corpus of documents (e.g., 100K documents). The benchmark pre-downloads all relevant documents, and the task requires associating information across several documents to answer queries. BrowseComp-Plus is used to demonstrate the scalability of Recursive Language Models (RLMs) in handling ridiculously large contexts (e.g., 10M+ tokens) without the need for retrieval tools like BM25.
