A probabilistic retrieval function used for ranking documents based on their relevance to a query. BM25 is commonly used in information retrieval tasks to retrieve top-k documents from a corpus. In the context of Recursive Language Models (RLMs), BM25 is compared as a baseline for handling long-context tasks, but RLMs are shown to outperform it by directly processing large contexts without explicit retrieval.
