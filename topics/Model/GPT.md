A family of large language models developed by OpenAI, including versions such as GPT-4 and the proprietary GPT-5.5, which are noted for their advanced capabilities in generating human-like text, understanding complex instructions, and serving as benchmarks in AI evaluations like the smevals suite. The family also includes GPT-5, a frontier LLM model evaluated in studies as an LLM judge, tested across multiple datasets and pressure levels to assess its epistemic stability and susceptibility to adversarial conditions.
