A family of large language models developed by OpenAI, including variants such as GPT-4, GPT-5, and the smaller GPT-5-mini. The family is noted for advanced capabilities in generating human-like text, understanding complex instructions, and serving as benchmarks in AI evaluations like the smevals suite. GPT-5-mini, a smaller variant, has been shown to outperform GPT-5 on the OOLONG benchmark by over double the number of correct answers while being cheaper per query on average. RLMs using GPT-5-mini leverage recursive calls to mitigate 'context rot' and handle long contexts more effectively.
