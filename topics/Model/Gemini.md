A family of multimodal models developed by Google, trained jointly across image, audio, video, and text data, enabling strong generalist capabilities and cutting-edge reasoning performance. The family includes versions such as Gemini 1.5, Gemini 1.5 Flash, Gemini 1.5 Pro, Ultra, Pro, and Nano, with notable advancements like 1M (later 2M) token input context length and video processing capabilities. The Gemini API provides developers with a platform to access and integrate these models, including specialized variants like Gemini Robotics ER 2 for real-time spatial reasoning, multi-step task planning, and robotics control. The VERDAD project utilizes Gemini 1.5 for transcribing audio and identifying misinformation, while Gemini 1.5 Pro has surpassed GPT-4 in capabilities.
