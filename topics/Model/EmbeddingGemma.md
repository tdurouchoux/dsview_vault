EmbeddingGemma is a family of open embedding models designed for on-device AI, featuring around 300M parameters. These models are optimized for tasks like Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG), semantic search, and generating high-quality, private embeddings that work offline. Built on the Gemma 3 architecture, they support multilingual text embedding and are suitable for a wide range of applications on everyday devices such as mobile phones, laptops, and desktops. The family includes variants like the 300M-parameter model, distributed as a GGUF quantized model for efficient deployment.
