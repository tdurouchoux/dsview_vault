A family of large language models developed by Anthropic, designed for generating and processing text, including AI text watermarking in newer versions. The family includes variants such as Claude Sonnet, which offers significant improvements in coding, reasoning, and math, excelling in agentic coding, tool use, and handling complex multi-step processes with enhanced reasoning and efficiency. This includes frontier models like Claude 3.7, evaluated by METR for capabilities, risks, and societal impact, particularly in autonomous agent behavior and safety.
