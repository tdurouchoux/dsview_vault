A family of advanced time series foundation models trained on large-scale time series data to provide a foundation for various forecasting tasks. These models are designed to be versatile and applicable across a wide range of forecasting problems, capturing underlying patterns and dependencies in time series data. Unlike traditional statistical models or task-specific deep learning models, TSFMs are trained once on diverse time series data and can be applied to different forecasting scenarios without task-specific training. TimesFM (Time Series Foundation Model), developed by Google Research, is a pretrained instance of this family, supporting univariate and multivariate forecasting (including iterative versions like TimesFM-2.5 and TimesFM-3) with features such as continuous quantile forecasting, parameter reduction, and increased context length. TSFMs are integrated into platforms like Google's BigQuery as official products.
