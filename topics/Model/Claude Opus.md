A high-performance tier of Anthropic's Claude model family, designed for advanced capabilities in tasks such as cybersecurity evaluations, coding, software engineering, and complex problem-solving. The Opus series is noted for its efficiency, ability to handle ambiguity, superior performance in multi-step reasoning tasks, and robustness against prompt injection attacks and other safety concerns. It is available on major cloud platforms and integrated into various applications and developer tools. Specific versions include Opus 4.5, Opus 4.6 Opus (a high-performance LLM model evaluated for mechanical consistency, single-turn conviction, and multi-turn persistence under the Wiggle Framework), and Opus 4.7.
