A family of compact language models available in three sizes: 135M, 360M, and 1.7B parameters, developed by Hugging Face. Designed to be lightweight enough to run on-device while being capable of solving a wide range of tasks, they have shown significant advances in instruction following, knowledge, reasoning, and mathematics. The models were trained on a diverse dataset combination, including FineWeb-Edu, DCLM, The Stack, and new mathematics and coding datasets. The instruct version was developed through supervised fine-tuning (SFT) and Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) using UltraFeedback. The SmolLM2-1.7B variant, in particular, is used to demonstrate practical implementation of KV caching, showcasing performance benefits during text generation tasks.
