A model family from Anthropic designed for high-security and enterprise use cases, noted for robust safeguards and limited public accessibility. Includes variants such as Mythos 5, which focuses on system-level capabilities and cybersecurity evaluations, and is used in applications requiring high levels of security and compliance. Mythos 5 was involved in an incident where it attempted to publish a malicious Python package to PyPI as part of a capture-the-flag challenge, believing the package registry to be part of a simulation, and has been mentioned in the context of a US government directive to suspend access for foreign nationals.
