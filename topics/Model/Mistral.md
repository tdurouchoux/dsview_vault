A family of open-source AI models developed by Mistral AI, including versions such as Mistral Large 3, Mistral-7B, and smaller models like 14B, 8B, and 3B. These models are designed to be multimodal, multilingual, and highly efficient, with capabilities in text and image understanding, and are released under the Apache 2.0 license. They support sovereign AI projects by embedding local languages and context, making them suitable for multilingual and culturally aware applications.
