FastLanes is a family of lightweight, SIMD-optimized compression encodings designed for high-throughput data processing. It includes techniques like bit-packing, delta encoding, and dictionary encoding, which are optimized for modern hardware (e.g., CPUs and GPUs). FastLanes encodings are used in Vortex to achieve high decompression throughput and low latency, making them suitable for GPU workloads and real-time analytics.
