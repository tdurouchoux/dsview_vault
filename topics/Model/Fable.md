A family of frontier AI models developed by Anthropic, designed for advanced reasoning and agentic tasks. The Fable line incorporates strengthened safeguards against misuse, particularly in cybersecurity, and is used in enterprise and high-security applications. Models in this line, such as Fable 5, have undergone extensive red-teaming by government agencies, private organizations, and internal teams to test resilience against jailbreaking attempts. Despite these efforts, non-universal (narrow) jailbreaks may exist. Anthropic employs a defense-in-depth strategy, including monitoring and customer data retention policies, to mitigate risks.
