Atlas is a multimodal autoregressive diffusion transformer developed by World Labs as a world model for spatial intelligence. It is designed to natively operate on text, images, video, and 3D data, combining inputs into a shared spatial context to generate, reconstruct, and simulate worlds. Atlas excels in tasks such as camera-controlled generation, spatial reconstruction, space-time simulation, and image generation, outperforming specialized models in benchmarks. Its architecture integrates multimodal processing, autoregressive generation, diffusion-based denoising, and transformer-based modeling, enabling scalable performance improvements with increased training compute.
