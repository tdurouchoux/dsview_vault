A family of large reasoning models developed by OpenAI, introduced in 2024, that use inference-scaling for reasoning by generating 'thinking tokens' or reasoning traces before outputting a final result. These models are designed for complex problem-solving, including mathematical tasks, and gained attention for solving a famous open mathematical research problem in one shot in May 2026, demonstrating the potential of LRMs in verifiable domains like mathematics.
