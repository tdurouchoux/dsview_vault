ULMFiT (Universal Language Model Fine-tuning) is a transfer learning method for NLP introduced in 2018. It involves pre-training a language model on a large corpus and then fine-tuning it on a target task, such as text classification. ULMFiT achieved state-of-the-art results on datasets like IMDb by leveraging pre-trained embeddings and fine-tuning strategies that improve generalization and reduce overfitting. The approach is particularly effective for smaller datasets.
