A family of large language models developed by the Qwen team, including variants such as Qwen3-235B (a Mixture-of-Experts model with 235 billion parameters), Qwen3-0.6B (a lightweight, efficient model with 0.6 billion parameters), and Qwen3.5-9B (a 9-billion-parameter base model designed for efficient decision scoring and single-pass inference). The Qwen3 series is designed to balance performance and resource requirements, with support for running larger variants on constrained hardware via techniques like layer streaming and model compression.
