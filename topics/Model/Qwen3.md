A family of large language models developed by the Qwen team, including variants such as Qwen3-235B (a Mixture-of-Experts model with 235 billion parameters) and Qwen3-0.6B (a lightweight, efficient model with 0.6 billion parameters). The Qwen3 series is designed to balance performance and resource requirements, with support for running larger variants on constrained hardware via techniques like layer streaming and model compression.
