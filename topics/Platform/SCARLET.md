SCARLET (Scam Cascade Analysis and Risk Learning Early Tracker) is a fraud-pressure intelligence and control-signalling system designed to operate at an institutional level. It converts fragmented fraud indicators into a clear control posture that scales with fraud pressure, enabling proactive tightening of controls based on upstream fraud signals rather than reactive case-by-case responses. The system is built around public complaint data and is tailored for a JPMorgan-style risk environment.
