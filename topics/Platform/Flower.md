Flower is an open-source framework designed for building federated AI systems. It enables collaborative machine learning across decentralized devices or servers while preserving data privacy. Flower is framework-agnostic, supporting integration with various ML frameworks such as PyTorch, TensorFlow, Hugging Face Transformers, and others. It emphasizes customizability, extensibility, and maintainability, making it suitable for both research and production environments.
