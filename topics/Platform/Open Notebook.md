Open Notebook is an open-source, AI-powered note-taking and research platform designed to enhance learning and knowledge retention while prioritizing user privacy. It allows users to integrate and manage various content types (e.g., links, PDFs, videos) and leverage AI for summarization, insights generation, and workflow customization. The platform emphasizes user control over data, AI models, and workflows, positioning itself as a 'cognitive partner' for researchers, students, and professionals.
