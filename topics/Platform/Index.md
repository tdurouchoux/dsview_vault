Index is a platform developed by Figure for collecting and curating large-scale, diverse physical datasets from real-world environments. It serves as a crowdsourced data collection system where users (Creators) upload videos of tasks performed in homes, workplaces, and other environments. The platform processes and structures this data for use in training AI models, particularly for robotics applications. Index emphasizes diversity, quality, and real-time feedback to ensure the dataset meets the needs of general-purpose robot training.
