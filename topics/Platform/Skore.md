Skore is a collaboration platform for data science teams, designed to help data scientists track, manage, and audit their machine learning experiments. It provides a comprehensive view of experiments and their results, enabling users to analyze, gain insights, and facilitate collaboration and decision-making in data science workflows. Skore integrates with libraries like scikit-learn and offers tools for model evaluation, cross-validation, benchmarking, and standardization of workflows. It aims to reduce boilerplate code, promote methodological best practices, and fit seamlessly into existing data science stacks without creating vendor lock-in.
