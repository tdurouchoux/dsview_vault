A Microsoft-hosted platform for hosting, deploying, and managing AI models, enabling developers to integrate models like Microsoft-Decision-1 into applications and workflows. It accelerates the development and deployment of AI models and applications, offering tools and services for building, training, deploying, and managing machine learning models (including traditional ML and generative AI). The platform integrates with external AI tools and services (e.g., Anthropic), supports seamless data processing, model evaluation, content safety, and real-time monitoring, and is designed to deploy AI solutions at scale with features for enterprise/consumer applications, model fine-tuning, deployment, monitoring, and risk management across the AI development lifecycle.
