GDELT (Global Database of Events, Language, and Tone) is a platform that monitors the world's news media in real-time, capturing global events, trends, and media-driven behaviors. In the context of SCARLET, GDELT's Doc 2.0 API is used to fetch weekly article counts related to JPMorgan fraud or Chase scams, serving as a secondary signal to capture media-driven filing behavior and compress the lag in fraud detection.
