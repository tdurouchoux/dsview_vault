Wishful mnemonics is a concept introduced by computer scientist Drew McDermott in 1976 to describe the tendency of researchers to use anthropomorphic or overly optimistic terminology to describe AI systems or components. This practice can lead to misconceptions about the capabilities or mechanisms of AI models. In the context of AI reasoning, terms like 'reasoning,' 'thinking,' or 'understanding' may be used wishfully, implying capabilities that are not scientifically validated.
