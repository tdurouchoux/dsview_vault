Control posture refers to the set of actions or monitoring levels an institution adopts in response to perceived risk or fraud pressure. In SCARLET, the control posture is determined by the regime classification (normal, elevated, or stressed) and can include actions such as monitor, heightened monitoring, pre-emptive tightening, or tighten controls. The control posture is designed to scale with the level of fraud pressure rather than reacting case by case.
