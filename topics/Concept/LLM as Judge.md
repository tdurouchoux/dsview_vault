The practice of using large language models (LLMs) to evaluate or grade outputs from other models or human-generated content. This approach is used in benchmarking, content moderation, reward modeling, and FACTS Grounding, where judges like Gemini 1.5 Pro, GPT-4o, and Claude 3.5 Sonnet assess factual accuracy and grounding of LLM responses. While effective, its reliability is questioned due to susceptibility to pressure and sycophancy.
