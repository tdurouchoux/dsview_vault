A computational approach where only the changes (deltas) in the data are processed and updated, rather than reprocessing the entire dataset. This method ensures efficiency, low latency, and cost-effectiveness, particularly in large-scale or real-time systems, by reusing unchanged data and only recalculating or reindexing what has been modified. It is foundational to modern streaming analytics, enabling systems to maintain derived results (e.g., materialized views) without recomputing from scratch, and is critical for dynamic environments requiring data freshness and system responsiveness, such as live context for AI agents or immediate integration of new data episodes into knowledge graphs. Frameworks like Timely Dataflow, Differential Dataflow, DBSP, and Enzyme formalize and optimize this approach for scalability and practicality in large-scale streaming workloads.
