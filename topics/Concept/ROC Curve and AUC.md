The Receiver Operating Characteristic (ROC) framework for binary classification, comprising:
- The ROC curve: a graphical plot illustrating a classifier’s diagnostic ability by showing the true positive rate (sensitivity) against the false positive rate (1-specificity) across varying thresholds.
- ROC-AUC: the area under the ROC curve, a scalar metric quantifying overall model discrimination between positive and negative classes (1 = perfect, 0.5 = random).
Used for model comparison but limited in low-prevalence scenarios and does not account for calibration.
