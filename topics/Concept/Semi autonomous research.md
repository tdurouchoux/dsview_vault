The practice of using AI agents to assist or automate parts of research workflows, such as data collection, hypothesis testing, experimentation, or ML model development. These agents operate autonomously or semi-autonomously, collaborating with human oversight to ensure alignment with goals, validation of results, and efficiency. Examples include systems like Claude Code, OpenCode, Cursor, and GitHub Copilot, which leverage tools (e.g., skore) to automate repetitive or computationally intensive tasks across research and machine learning domains.
