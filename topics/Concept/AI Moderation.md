The use of artificial intelligence to monitor, filter, and manage content (including text, images, and combinations thereof) to ensure compliance with guidelines and policies. This encompasses unified safety assessments across modalities, such as detecting and removing inappropriate, harmful, or non-compliant content in applications like social media, content platforms, or AI assistants. Systems like Shieldstral handle diverse input types (e.g., prompts, responses, images with optional text) to provide comprehensive moderation.
