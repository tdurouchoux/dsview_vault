Geometric Deep Learning is a subfield of deep learning that focuses on developing models for data structured as geometric objects, such as graphs, meshes, and point clouds. It extends traditional deep learning techniques to non-Euclidean domains, enabling the modeling of relational and irregular data structures. Geometric deep learning includes methods such as Graph Neural Networks (GNNs), Graph Convolutional Networks (GCNs), and Graph Attention Networks (GATs).
