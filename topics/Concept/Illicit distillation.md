Illicit distillation refers to the unauthorized extraction and replication of a model's capabilities, particularly its reasoning traces, by third parties. This process involves harvesting exchanges between users and a frontier model (e.g., Claude) to train smaller or competing models without permission. Illicit distillation is a significant threat to AI providers, as it enables unauthorized entities to replicate advanced capabilities at a fraction of the cost and time required for independent development.
