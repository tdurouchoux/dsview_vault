The presence of training, fine-tuning, or evaluation-related data within benchmark datasets or accessible resources of AI models, leading to unreliable, biased, or inflated performance metrics. This issue undermines the integrity of benchmarking efforts and model assessments, as it may not reflect true real-world capabilities. The problem is particularly acute in scenarios like time series forecasting, where historical data correlations can indirectly leak information, and preventing contamination often involves restricting access to specific domains or sources during evaluation to ensure fair and valid assessments.
