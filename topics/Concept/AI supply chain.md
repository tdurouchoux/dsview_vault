The AI supply chain encompasses the infrastructure, tools, and services used to develop, deploy, and operate AI models. In the context of the report, the AI supply chain is a deliberate target for threat actors, who seek to compromise AI API keys, session tokens, and integrations to gain unauthorized access to AI capabilities. The supply chain includes resellers, proxy networks, and third-party routing services, which are exploited to evade safeguards and harvest valuable data.
