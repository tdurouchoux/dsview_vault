The practice of monitoring, logging, and analyzing the behavior and internal state of AI systems (including agents, coding agents, and Large Language Models) throughout their lifecycle. This encompasses tracking user interactions, context retrieval, tool calls, decision-making processes, and model outputs to identify failures, inefficiencies, biases, or compliance risks. Observability tools provide capabilities such as trace visualization, automated evaluation, and real-time monitoring to ensure systems perform as expected, meet quality standards, and adhere to governance policies. Agent-Native Observability, where telemetry and observability tools are integrated directly into coding agents or AI-driven workflows, is a key application of this practice, enabling agents to leverage production context for incident investigation, alert tuning, and dashboard building.
