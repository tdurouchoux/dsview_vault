The field focused on ensuring that artificial intelligence systems are beneficial, safe, and do not pose risks to humanity. This includes formalized guidelines and practices such as third-party audits, transparency requirements, and capability limits to mitigate risks. AI safety involves evaluating the safety, security, and risks of advanced AI systems through international collaboration, technical solutions, ethical guidelines, and regulatory frameworks. It encompasses techniques and policies to prevent harm from unintended actions, misuse, or malicious intent, while fostering cooperation, sharing best practices, and conducting joint evaluations to enhance global AI safety and trustworthiness. This includes addressing issues such as bias, privacy, security, societal impact, transparency, fairness, and respect for human rights in AI development and deployment. Frontier AI Safety Policies refer to shared components and methodologies across safety policies published by leading AI companies, including capability thresholds, model weight security, and deployment mitigations.
