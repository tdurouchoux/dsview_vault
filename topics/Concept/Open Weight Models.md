AI models whose weights (parameters) are publicly available, allowing users to fine-tune, modify, and deploy them without relying on proprietary APIs or licensing restrictions. This includes open LLMs (Large Language Models) released under licenses such as Apache 2.0, MIT, or OpenRAIL-M, which are designed for commercial use, research, and customization. Open-weight models enable greater flexibility and control over AI systems, often used in sovereign AI projects to embed local languages and cultural context, and are frequently accompanied by datasets and training codes to facilitate further development.
