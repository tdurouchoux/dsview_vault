A unified stress test designed to evaluate the epistemic stability of LLM judges under various forms of pressure. It decomposes judge robustness into three dimensions: Mechanical Consistency (stability under re-prompting and semantically invariant prompt variations), Single-turn Conviction (stability under a single substantive challenge), and Multi-turn Persistence (stability under sustained or adaptive pressure). The framework measures wiggle rates (fraction of verdict changes from a baseline) and classifies flips as corrective or corruptive relative to ground truth.
