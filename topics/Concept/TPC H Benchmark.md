The TPC-H Benchmark, developed by the Transaction Processing Performance Council (TPC), is a decision-support benchmark suite designed to measure the performance of relational database management systems (RDBMS) and data processing frameworks. It consists of a suite of business-oriented ad-hoc queries and concurrent data modifications, evaluating a system's ability to handle complex queries on large datasets. Metrics include query execution time and throughput, and it is widely used in the industry to assess the performance of database systems and data processing frameworks.
