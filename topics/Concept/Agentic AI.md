Agentic AI, also referred to as AI Agents, represents artificial intelligence systems designed to operate autonomously or semi-autonomously, making decisions and executing tasks to achieve specific goals with minimal human intervention. These systems leverage advanced techniques such as reinforcement learning, planning, tool-use, and language models to interact with their environment, solve complex problems, and adapt to dynamic conditions.

Agentic AI systems can function as assistants, coworkers, or coaches, performing a wide range of tasks such as managing emails, calendars, automating workflows, data gathering, market research, and customer account management. They are characterized by their ability to understand user intent, interact with digital environments, and retain context over time (persistent memory). These agents integrate seamlessly with various platforms and tools, operating across multiple interfaces like chat apps (e.g., WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord) and extending their functionality through plugins or custom skills.

Agentic AI is particularly relevant in domains requiring autonomous decision-making, such as robotics, customer service automation, and workflow optimization. They can range from simple chatbots to complex systems capable of autonomous planning, learning, and task execution, often operating through a cycle of thoughts, actions, and observations. Frameworks like LangGraph, LlamaIndex, and smolagents are commonly used to streamline their development.

These systems are ideal for open-ended problems where the required steps are unpredictable, making them suitable for scaling tasks in trusted environments. They can operate independently for private, offline tasks or as part of larger connected systems, handling common commands at the edge and routing more complex tasks to larger models. Extensive testing and appropriate guardrails are essential for their reliable deployment.
