The process of identifying, parsing, and organizing unstructured or semi-structured data (e.g., from documents like PDFs or images) into a structured, machine-readable format such as tables, CSV, or JSON. This includes techniques like rectangle-based detection from PDF drawing operations or heuristic detection from text alignment, and is critical for applications requiring precise data retrieval (e.g., financial reports, scientific papers, or legal documents). It involves handling challenges like merged cells, hierarchical headers, and complex layouts to ensure accurate reconstruction of the original structure, and is essential for data analysis and integration workflows.
