A technique for generating machine-readable, structured responses from models, where responses are organized in predictable formats (e.g., JSON, tables, or predefined templates) to enable programmatic processing. This includes typed answers (e.g., probabilities, choices, or scores) based on predefined questions or constraints, contrasting with unstructured outputs that require additional parsing or natural language processing. Supported by models like gpt-oss-safeguard-120b and gpt-oss-safeguard-20b, and achievable using parameters such as struct and struct_descr to specify desired formats and constraints.
