A structured approach to identify, assess, and mitigate risks associated with AI systems, developed by organizations like NIST and METR. It includes processes for governing, mapping, measuring, and managing AI risks throughout the AI lifecycle, such as independent reviews of AI developers' risk assessments, capability evaluations, and investigations into AI incidents. The framework ensures AI is developed and deployed safely, securely, and in alignment with ethical principles.
