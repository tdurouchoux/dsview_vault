A method of tracking requests or execution flows as they traverse systems, capturing and analyzing the journey of a request across multiple components (e.g., microservices or LLM application steps). It involves visualizing the flow using tools like flamegraphs and waterfalls, and identifying performance bottlenecks or failures. Tracing is essential for debugging, performance optimization, and ensuring transparency in distributed or AI-driven workflows, and can be implemented asynchronously to avoid blocking application execution.
