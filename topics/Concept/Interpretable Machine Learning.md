A subfield of machine learning focused on developing and applying techniques to make the decisions and predictions of complex models (including deep learning) understandable and explainable to humans. This involves designing inherently interpretable models and using post-hoc methods to explain the behavior of more complex models, with a focus on transparency, trust, accountability, and regulatory compliance. The science of understanding and explaining the internal workings of AI models—akin to tools like fMRI scans for AI 'brains'—helps identify misalignments, unexpected behaviors, and underlying causes of model decisions.
