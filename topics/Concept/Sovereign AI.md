Sovereign AI, also referred to as Technological and AI Sovereignty, is the development, control, and governance of AI technologies by individual organizations, nations, or regions. It emphasizes local ownership of AI models, data, and infrastructure to ensure independence, autonomy, and alignment with local priorities, regulations, and values. This concept aims to reduce reliance on foreign or corporate-controlled AI systems, fostering self-sufficiency in critical technological domains, ensuring data security, and promoting homegrown innovation. It is central to initiatives like national AI strategies and state-backed investments in AI supercomputing.
