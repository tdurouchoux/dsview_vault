An approach in machine learning where models are designed to explicitly represent and utilize high-level, interpretable concepts (e.g., features or latent variables) that bridge the gap between raw data and final predictions. This includes analyzing feature activations to determine what concepts or behaviors they represent, assessing their specificity and influence, and using automated interpretability methods to provide explanations and ensure extracted features are meaningful and useful for transparent and explainable model behavior.
