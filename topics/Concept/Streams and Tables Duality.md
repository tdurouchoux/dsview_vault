A foundational concept in streaming analytics that recognizes streams (ordered sequences of changes) and tables (point-in-time snapshots of data) as two representations of the same underlying object. This duality enables systems to seamlessly convert between streams and tables, enabling both incremental updates and point-in-time queries. It underpins modern streaming systems, where a stream of changes can be materialized into a table, and a table can be queried as a stream of snapshots.
