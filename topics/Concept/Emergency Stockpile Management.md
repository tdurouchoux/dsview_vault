Emergency stockpile management involves the strategic planning and utilization of stored resources (e.g., crude oil, food, medical supplies) to mitigate the impact of disruptions in supply chains. This includes determining optimal stockpile levels, monitoring depletion rates during crises, and ensuring that critical sectors (e.g., healthcare, energy) have access to necessary resources. The goal is to prevent shortages and stabilize markets during periods of high stress.
