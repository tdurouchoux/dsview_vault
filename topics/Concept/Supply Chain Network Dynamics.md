Supply Chain Network Dynamics refers to the study of how supply chains behave as complex systems, where the interactions between nodes (e.g., suppliers, manufacturers, distributors) and flows (e.g., goods, information, capital) determine the overall resilience, efficiency, and responsiveness of the network. This field analyzes how disruptions propagate through the network, how inventory and capacity constraints affect operations, and how pricing mechanisms adjust based on supply and demand imbalances. It often involves modeling techniques such as fluid-stochastic network clearing, inventory dynamics, and strategic trade rebalancing to simulate real-world scenarios.
