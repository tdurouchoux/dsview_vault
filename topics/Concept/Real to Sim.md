Real-to-Sim (Real-to-Simulation) is a paradigm that involves reconstructing real-world environments in a simulated space to enable training, testing, and evaluation of AI systems like robots. By simulating sensor data (e.g., RGB and depth images) from real-world recordings, models can generate diverse training data and test scenarios without requiring physical setups. Atlas facilitates Real-to-Sim workflows by reconstructing 3D scenes and simulating robot interactions within those environments.
