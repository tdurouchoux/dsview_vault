AI systems or internal representations designed to simulate environments, predict outcomes, and enable planning. These models learn the underlying structure of environments to generate, reconstruct, and simulate possible worlds, simulate physical interactions, and plan actions. They operate on multimodal inputs (e.g., text, images, video, and 3D data) and use spatial context to maintain consistency in generated outputs, allowing agents to reason about future actions and adapt to dynamic environments.
