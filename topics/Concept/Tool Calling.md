A capability of language models that enables them to interact with external tools, APIs, or functions by generating structured calls to retrieve information, execute code, or perform actions. This feature bridges language understanding and real-world applications, allowing models to integrate with systems like weather APIs, code execution environments, or other services in a controlled and predictable manner. The model processes returned data to produce final responses, supporting agentic workflows (e.g., Kolibri-1) and enhancing versatility beyond text generation.
