Practices and environments for evaluating the effectiveness of security tools, models, or processes, including standardized tests, datasets, and controlled attack simulations (evaluation ranges). These benchmarks measure performance in areas such as vulnerability detection, exploit development, and threat mitigation, providing a quantitative basis for comparing approaches and ensuring real-world security improvements. Controlled environments (evaluation ranges) are isolated and monitored to simulate realistic cyberattack scenarios for assessing AI model capabilities and safety.
