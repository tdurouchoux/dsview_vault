Approximate retrieval is a hypothesis proposed by Subbarao Kambhampati to explain the behavior of LRMs. It suggests that these models perform a form of pattern matching or retrieval from their training corpus, rather than engaging in true step-by-step reasoning. The 'thinking tokens' generated by LRMs are posited to load the model's context window in a way that makes it more likely to predict reasoning-shaped outputs, akin to 'mumbling' to jog one's memory.
