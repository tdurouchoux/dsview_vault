Specialized large language models (LLMs) designed to excel at complex tasks requiring multi-step reasoning, such as solving puzzles, advanced math problems, and coding challenges. These models generate intermediate reasoning steps (e.g., 'chains of thought' or 'thinking tokens') to mimic human-like reasoning processes, optimizing for structured, step-by-step outputs. While effective for tasks demanding detailed thought processes, they may incur higher computational costs and be prone to overthinking compared to standard LLMs. Their internal mechanisms and the faithfulness of their reasoning traces remain subjects of ongoing debate.
