A scenario in binary classification where the proportion of positive cases (e.g., disease presence) is very low compared to negative cases. Low prevalence can lead to misleading performance metrics like ROC-AUC, where high sensitivity and specificity may hide poor positive predictive value (PPV) due to a high number of false positives. It highlights the importance of evaluating models using metrics like PPV, NPV, and decision-focused methods like DCA.
