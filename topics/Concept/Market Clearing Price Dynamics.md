Market clearing price dynamics describe how prices adjust in response to changes in supply and demand within a market. In the context of supply chains, this involves modeling how prices fluctuate when disruptions occur, such as reduced capacity, increased demand, or logistical bottlenecks. Endogenous pricing mechanisms account for strategic behavior by market participants, such as rebalancing trade flows or adjusting inventory levels, to restore equilibrium in the system.
