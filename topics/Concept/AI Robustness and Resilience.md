The ability of AI systems to perform reliably under adverse conditions, including adversarial attacks, distribution shifts, and unexpected inputs, while maintaining consistent judgments or beliefs (epistemic stability). This encompasses the capacity to recover from failures, maintain functionality in dynamic environments, and uphold robust verdicts despite challenges such as re-prompting, adversarial persuasion, or consensus fabrication—particularly relevant for LLM judges and other high-stakes applications.
