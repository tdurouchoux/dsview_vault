A parameter or setting that controls the level of deliberation or cognitive processing a model applies to generate an answer, allowing adjustment between response quality and latency or cost. This feature is used in models like Kolibri-1 and platforms like the Claude Developer Platform to optimize performance based on task requirements, with options such as low, medium, high, or disabled effort levels. Higher reasoning effort typically results in more detailed and accurate responses but may increase latency.
