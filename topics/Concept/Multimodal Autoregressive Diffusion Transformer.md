A multimodal autoregressive diffusion transformer is a type of AI model that combines multimodal processing, autoregressive generation, and diffusion-based denoising within a transformer architecture. Such models process sequences of multimodal data (e.g., text, images, and 3D inputs) and generate outputs conditioned on a shared context. The diffusion component enables high-quality generation of continuous data like images and video, while the transformer backbone ensures scalability and efficiency.
