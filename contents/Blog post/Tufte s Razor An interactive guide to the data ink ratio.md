## Summary

Tufte's Razor is an interactive guide explaining the data-ink ratio principle for effective data visualization.

**Core Concept**
- Data-ink ratio: proportion of ink used to display data vs. total ink in a graphic.
- Higher ratio = better visualization (less non-data ink).

**Key Insights**
- Remove redundant or decorative elements to maximize data-ink.
- Focus on clarity and direct data representation.
- Examples show how to simplify charts by eliminating unnecessary gridlines, borders, or colors.
