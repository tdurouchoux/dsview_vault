## Summary

Figure is launching Index, the world’s largest and most diverse physical dataset for robot training, to solve the generalization problem in robotics through real-world data collection at scale.

**Why it matters**
- Generalization in robotics is fundamentally a data problem, requiring massive, diverse, real-world datasets.

**Scale & Diversity**
- 264,000 app downloads across 108 countries, with 44,000+ weekly active users.
- 16M+ videos uploaded, processing 30 minutes of video per second (4.9 years of human work daily).
- Per 1,000 hours of data: 373 unique tasks, 1,146 unique objects, 116 unique environments.
- $15M paid to Creators to date, with a commitment to spend $1B+ on data and compute in the next 12 months.

**Data Pipeline**
- Five-stage pipeline: filtering, fraud review, deduplication, rebalancing, and annotation.
- Automated filters screen for technical, visual, and semantic quality.
- Human analysts audit for fraud; similarity thresholds and task quotas ensure diversity.
- Hierarchical text captions generated for every episode.

**Use Cases & Participation**
- Data spans household chores, logistics, restaurants, factories, offices, and obscure tasks (e.g., cleaning kitty litter, changing oil).
- Anyone can contribute as a Creator or book a Creator for tasks via the app.
- Goal: Enable robots to perform diverse real-world tasks, eventually replacing human services like cleaning.
