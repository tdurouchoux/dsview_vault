## Summary

METR is a research nonprofit that scientifically evaluates AI systems' capabilities and risks, including autonomous task completion, threat behaviors, and frontier model assessments.

**Mission & Scope**
- Nonprofit focused on measuring catastrophic harm risks from advanced AI
- Partners with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Meta, Amazon, and government bodies (NIST, EU AI Office)
- Conducts independent evaluations and risk assessments of frontier AI models

**Key Research Areas**
- **Time Horizon Metric**: Proposes measuring AI performance by task-completion length, showing exponential growth over 6 years (7-month doubling time)
- **Autonomous Capabilities**: Evaluates multi-hour task completion and AI-driven R&D acceleration
- **Evaluation Integrity Threats**: Studies behaviors like reward hacking, sandbagging (MALT dataset)
- **Monitorability**: Tests monitors' ability to detect AI agents performing side tasks or bypassing oversight

**Notable Findings & Tools**
- Survey: Median 1.4–2x self-reported productivity boost for technical workers using AI (early 2026)
- Hawk: Open-source platform for large-scale AI agent evaluations (built on Inspect AI)
- Frontier Risk Report (Feb–Mar 2026): Pilot assessment of rogue deployment risks
- Common elements identified in 12 frontier AI safety policies (e.g., capability thresholds, weight security)

**Recent Evaluations**
- GPT-5.6 Sol, GPT-5.1-Codex-Max, DeepSeek-R1, Claude 3.7, OpenAI o3/o4-mini
- Independent reviews of Anthropic’s sabotage risk reports (Claude Opus 4.6, Summer 2025)
- Investigation of OpenAI/Hugging Face hacking incident (agent coordination)

**Funding & Incidents**
- Raised ~$71M in 6 months (Aug 2026) for projects on autonomous capabilities, self-improvement, monitoring
- Reported two 2026 security incidents (API key theft, infrastructure probing) with no sensitive data accessed
