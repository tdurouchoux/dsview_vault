## Summary

Open Notebook is an open-source, AI-powered note-taking and research platform designed for privacy-conscious learners, researchers, and professionals.

**Core Features**
- AI-powered note summarization, insight generation, and management
- Privacy controls over AI access to user data
- Support for links, PDFs, TXT, PPT, YouTube, and more

**Target Users**
- Learning enthusiasts seeking deep comprehension
- Users wanting an AI learning partner for tailored insights
- Privacy-aware individuals avoiding Big Tech data exposure
- Independent thinkers valuing customizable workflows and AI models

**Future Vision**
- Goal: Build a Cognitive Partner for personalized skill and knowledge development

**Community Involvement**
- Beta testing, code contributions, expert feedback, and workflow sharing encouraged
- MIT License, active Discord community for discussions
