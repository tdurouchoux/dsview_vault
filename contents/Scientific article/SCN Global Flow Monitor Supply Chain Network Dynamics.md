## Summary

The SCN Global Flow Monitor models global crude oil trade dynamics using a fluid-stochastic network approach with endogenous pricing and strategic trade rebalancing. Key technical points include:

- **Model Framework**: Uses Skorokhod inventory dynamics and network clearing to simulate supply chain disruptions.
- **Scenarios**: Tests historical analogs (70% probability, 12 weeks), severe disruptions (30%, 26 weeks), and extreme stress tests (10%, 52 weeks).
- **Parameters**: Fixed capacity retention (30%), demand elasticity (0.10), and supply elasticity (0.10) over a 26-week horizon.
- **Shock Targets**: Collapse (>60%), degraded (10–60%), or insulated flows, with overland/pipeline infrastructure adjustments.
- **Outputs**: Tracks market price, downstream flow losses, and emergency stockpile drawdowns (net-importers only; exporters use elastic-supply buffers).
- **Assumptions**: No rationing policy; buffers drain mechanically. Data sourced from UN Comtrade 2025 (sanctioned/unreported trade excluded).
- **Tool**: Interactive simulation for operations/analysis teams; contact@staffinganalytics.io for inquiries.
