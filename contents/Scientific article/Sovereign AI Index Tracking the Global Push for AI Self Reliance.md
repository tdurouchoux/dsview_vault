## Summary

The Sovereign AI Index tracks global government-backed AI initiatives aimed at reducing dependence on U.S. and Chinese AI dominance, revealing a surge in projects but persistent reliance on foreign (mostly U.S.) technology.

**Global Trends**
- 184 sovereign AI projects tracked, with 59% focused on infrastructure, 32% on models, and 9% on data.
- Infrastructure projects (e.g., data centers, supercomputers) have accelerated sharply, surpassing model projects in early 2026.
- Middle East and East Asia account for >80% of disclosed sovereign AI investment, with UAE and Japan alone contributing ~66%.

**Dependencies & Challenges**
- >60% of projects involve at least one foreign partner, with 80% of these including a U.S. company (e.g., NVIDIA, AWS, Oracle).
- NVIDIA GPUs power 45% of infrastructure projects; U.S. firms dominate across chips, servers, cloud, and networking.
- Most countries fine-tune open-weight models (e.g., Meta’s Llama) rather than build from scratch, still relying on U.S. foundations.

**Key Insights**
- Sovereign AI is more about managing dependencies than eliminating them, with countries prioritizing control over select stack layers.
- Full AI independence is unattainable even for advanced economies due to global supply chain interdependencies.
- Non-U.S. alternatives (e.g., France’s Mistral, China’s open models) exist but lack comprehensive stack coverage.
