## Summary

Flower (`flwr`) is a customizable, framework-agnostic federated learning (FL) framework designed for research and production use.

**Core principles**
- Customizable: adapts to diverse FL use cases
- Extendable: components can be overridden for new FL systems
- Framework-agnostic: works with PyTorch, TensorFlow, JAX, scikit-learn, Hugging Face, XGBoost, etc.
- Understandable: maintainable codebase, open to community contributions

**Key features**
- Supports vertical FL, federated finetuning (LLMs, ViTs, Whisper), and embedded devices (Raspberry Pi, Jetson)
- Provides pre-built baselines for 20+ FL algorithms (FedAvg, FedProx, FedBN, MOON, etc.)
- Includes quickstart examples for 15+ ML frameworks/libraries
- Offers tutorials on FL fundamentals, strategies, customization, and privacy/security

**Resources**
- Active community (Slack, GitHub)
- Comprehensive [docs](https://flower.ai/docs) and [baselines](https://flower.ai/docs/baselines/)
- Citable research framework ([arXiv:2007.14390](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.14390))
