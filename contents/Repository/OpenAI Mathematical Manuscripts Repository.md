## Summary

This repository shares raw mathematical research outputs generated by an internal OpenAI model, offering insights into AI-driven mathematical discovery and verification.

**Content**
- 721 manuscripts grouped into 373 families, each classified by mathematical discipline.
- ~42% of top-line results have Lean formalizations, with more to be added.
- Includes PDFs, source files, and build instructions in the `preprints/` directory.
- Reasoning summaries available for 10 selected results (e.g., irrationality exponent of π, Kaplansky’s conjecture).

**Production**
- Most results generated using a fixed procedure with an unreleased OpenAI model.
- Average of 3 hours of ChatGPT Pro compute per result, from ~4,000 posed problems.
- Exceptions include human-edited work on Riemann zeta function and Hodge Conjecture.

**Structure**
- Overview and manuscript map provided for navigation.
- Lean library and formalization catalogue detail available proofs.
- Updates tracked in `history.md`, with versioned citations for individual manuscripts.
