## Summary

This book is a free, web-based revision guide for data science interviews, covering key topics concisely rather than in depth.

**Purpose & Scope**
- Free, always-updating online resource for interview prep
- Covers statistics, ML, Python, SQL, business intelligence, and more
- Designed as revision notes, not a textbook
- Assumes prior familiarity with topics

**Content Highlights**
- **Statistics**: Probability, CLT, Bayesian vs Frequentist, hypothesis testing, A/B tests
- **Model Building**: Bias/variance tradeoff, regression, classification, clustering, time series, anomaly detection
- **Neural Networks**: RNNs, NLP (lexical/syntactic processing, transformers)
- **Tools**: Python (NumPy, Pandas, PySpark), SQL, Excel, PyCaret, TensorFlow
- **MLOps**: Git, feature stores, performance tuning
- **Business**: Power BI, visualization, industry applications
- **Cheat Sheets**: NumPy, Pandas, SQL, Git, etc.

**Contribution & Updates**
- Updated monthly with new content
- Open-source; accepts merge requests and forum contributions
- Supported via donations (Buy Me a Coffee)
